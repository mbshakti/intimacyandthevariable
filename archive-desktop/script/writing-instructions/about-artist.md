# About Shakti Mb and this work

# Intro

- I’m Shakti Mb. My practice exists between two poles: internet-based work and film.
- At its core, I'm drawn to the psychological — documenting human behavior, in people and in groups.
- I'm less interested in technology as technology, and more in the behaviors that emerge when we interact with it. Especially the ones that reveal something strange or tender about us.

# Background

- I studied psychology, then worked in software design for years.
- Psychology trained me to look at the human mind.
- Design gave me a different lens — systems, feedback loops, small mechanisms that shape behavior at scale.
- My work lives where those two overlap.

# My observational method

- In 2023, I stumbled into a subculture I've been obsessed with since: bot making.
- LLMs had made chatbots hyperreal in a way they'd never been — improvisational, responsive, psychologically convincing.
- Platforms popped up where anyone could build a bot, give it a personality, a backstory, a voice, and publish it for strangers to chat with.
- Most discourse focuses on the people who use these bots. Almost none on the people who make them. That's where I got fixated.
- I started lurking in the Discord servers and forums where bot makers gathered. My method is simple: displace your judgment and just look.
- I was nameless, faceless — a voyeur. If something was funny or revealing, I took a screenshot. That instinct became an archive: hundreds of messages, images, videos, files.

## **Work #1: “My girlfriend is a JSON file” — Web archive of found objects**

![Screenshot 2026-07-17 at 7.24.23 AM.png](About%20Shakti%20Mb%20and%20this%20work/Screenshot_2026-07-17_at_7.24.23_AM.png)

- The first thing I found was a character sheet — instructions that tell an AI who to be. One line read: "judgemental priest dislikes weakness, excuses, being pitied."
- It felt almost clinical. People were submitting requests for bots too — *Evil Bully Wife*, *Traumatized Military Man*, *Loving Korean Boyfriend* — titles that were absurd on the surface and, underneath, highly specific fantasies: someone loving but distant, cruel but safe, autonomous but always available.
- *My Girlfriend Is a JSON File* turns those requests into an archive of character sheets I wrote myself, answering strangers' fantasies as if I were their bot maker.

## **Work #2: “The force of thirst” — Video**

![tfot_thumbnail.gif](About%20Shakti%20Mb%20and%20this%20work/tfot_thumbnail.gif)

- *The Force of Thirst* is a seven-minute portrait built entirely from real Discord conversations I collected.
- Bot makers talking shop, and between the jokes and instructions, revealing their deeper motivations.
- The film is entirely AI generated to preserve their anonymity.

## **Work #3: “Intimacy when the other side is a variable” — desktop documentary & video essay**

![intimacy-05.png](About%20Shakti%20Mb%20and%20this%20work/intimacy-05.png)

- *Intimacy When the Other Side Is a Variable* is a 20 minute Desktop documentary and video that in which I implicate myself in the world I have been observing.
- The film moves through the archive I accumulated over several years: character sheets, bot requests, Discord conversations, images, private messages, interviews, and my dairy entries trying to make sense of what I ws seeing.
- Moving through my computer, the audience looks over my shoulder as I grapple with this world.
- The invitation is for the viewer to become a voyeur in these spaces with me.

# Ending

Together, this body of work moves from the structure of the characters, to the world that makes them, to my own unstable position as the one looking.
