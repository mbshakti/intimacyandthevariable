# Film vs. Talk Writing Guide

## Talk
**Purpose:** Explain the work.

- Speak from outside the project.
- Connect observations into ideas.
- Give context and orientation.
- Name concepts when needed.
- Arrive at conclusions.

**Question:** "What did I learn?"

---

## Film
**Purpose:** Let the audience discover the work.

- Speak from inside the investigation.
- Stay close to what is happening on screen.
- Describe before interpreting.
- Trust the desktop to tell half the story.
- Let conclusions emerge naturally.

**Question:** "What am I noticing?"

---

## Desktop Documentary Film Writing Rules

- Follow the cursor.
- React to what you see.
- Stay with the object before jumping to the idea.
- Explain less than you know.
- If the desktop already communicates something, don't repeat it in narration.
- Let pauses, clicks, scrolling, and hesitation carry meaning.

---

## Litmus Test

If the sentence could appear in an artist statement, it belongs in the talk.

If the sentence only makes sense because of what the audience is watching on the screen right now, it belongs in the film.

---

## Voice

Start with something ordinary.

A desktop.
A folder.
A filename.
A cursor.
A screenshot.

Stay with the object longer than feels necessary.

Let it become meaningful through attention, not explanation.

Favor observations over conclusions.

Instead of explaining what something means, describe it carefully enough that the audience begins to feel its meaning.

Move from the concrete to the psychological.

Don't force the insight.

Leave a little unsaid.

## Sentence Test

Good:

"Research doesn't always look like research."

"Eventually, I knew where everything was."

"Not because it was organized."

"Because I'd wandered through it so many times."

Less interesting:

"This made me realize..."

"This represents..."

"This symbolizes..."

"The point is..."

Trust the audience to make the connection.

## Follow Attention

Write about what held your attention.

Not what was objectively important.

If you kept reopening a folder, ask why.

If a filename stayed with you, ask why.

If you couldn't delete a screenshot, ask why.

Your curiosity is part of the story.
