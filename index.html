overview

I lurk in the online communities where people make AI roleplay bots. A botmaker named Delta draws me in. This film is an invitation to be a voyeur in these spaces with me.

Unstable Performers is an audiovisual performance that turns the audience into voyeurs, lurking in the online space where AI botmakers gather. The film Intimacy when the other side is a variable is staged live, with me reading from my diary in the room.

Shakti Mb

Shakti Mb Director, co-editorartist, filmmaker

Shakti Mb is a New York based artist, filmmaker, and software designer whose work explores the psychology of people. Her work has been supported by Rhizome, NEW INC, Emergent Ventures, Mozilla, and UnionDocs, and presented at the New Museum, Anthology Film Archives, and DCTV. Her net-art archive, My Girlfriend Is a JSON File, received a 2024 Rhizome Microgrant. She is currently a NEW INC Y13 member and was previously a NEW INC Art + Code Fellow, Mozilla Counter Structures resident, and UnionDocs Collaborative Studio Fellow.

Kiana Fernandez

Kiana Fernandez Co-editor, creative directionartist, filmmaker, exhibition designer

Kiana Fernandez is an artist, art director, multimedia designer, and filmmaker based in New York City.

Engaging across the boundaries of print, video, photomontage, crowdsourcing, and tangibility, her work has been exhibited through Anthology Film Archives, The Museum of Moving Image, The New Museum, Union Docs, The MOMA, Nguyen Wahed, DCTV, Rockaway Film Festival, Science New Wave Festival, and more.
