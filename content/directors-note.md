Many people are forming emotional attachments to AI chatbots. This is uncomfortable enough that most discourse is either dystopic (the world is ending!), or utopic dismissal(technology is the solution to loneliness!). 

The documentary impulse, as I understand it, is to displace judgment and you look, without deciding what it means.

This film is three years of me lurking in the online spaces where people build AI roleplay bots, and is an invitation for you to be a voyeur in these spaces with me.
