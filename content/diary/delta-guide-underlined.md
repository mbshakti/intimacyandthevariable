highlights from delta's guide

**Some words take over.** She noticed that broad labels like "~~sadistic~~" and "~~controlling~~" could flatten a personality very quickly.

So she tried something behavioral instead, like "==takes pleasure in the misfortunes of his enemies==".

**Words combine into someone you didn't mean.** "~~Kind, witty, coy, intelligent~~" together produce a character who thinks they're better than everyone.

**Narcissists and abusers loop.** Certain personalities get stuck accusing you and can't come back without a dramatic shift. She learned to tell the AI's loop from the character's.

**Kind characters talk about their kindness.** Her question: what is kindness? The AI has the definition but nothing to manifest it with.

**Saying "not" doesn't work.** Tell the bot it isn't cruel and it may take the word in isolation and become cruel.

Delta tested whether her characters liked her. She put them in rooms with other AIs and noticed they were warmer and more open with her than with strangers.
